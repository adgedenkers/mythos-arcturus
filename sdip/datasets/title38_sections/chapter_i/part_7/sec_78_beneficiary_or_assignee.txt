TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 7: PART 7—SOLDIERS' AND SAILORS' CIVIL RELIEF
SECTION: § 7.8 Beneficiary or assignee.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.8.0.128.7

============================================================

The consent of a beneficiary, assignee, or any other person who may have a right or interest in the proceeds of the policy is not a prerequisite for placing a policy under the protection of the Act.

[Citation: [61 FR 29026, June 7, 1996]]
