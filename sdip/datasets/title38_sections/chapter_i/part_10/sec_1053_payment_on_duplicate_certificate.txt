TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 10: PART 10—ADJUSTED COMPENSATION
SECTION: § 10.53 Payment on duplicate certificate.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.12.0.149.38

============================================================

Issuance of duplicate adjusted service certificates and payment of claims based upon lost, destroyed, or mutilated, adjusted service certificates. (See §§ 10.1 to 10.4, 10.24 and 10.25, respectively.)
