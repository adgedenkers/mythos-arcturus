TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 10: PART 10—ADJUSTED COMPENSATION
SECTION: § 10.4 Loss, destruction, or mutilation of adjusted service certificate while in possession of Department of Veterans Affairs.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.12.0.148.5

============================================================

A new adjusted service certificate will be issued without bond in lieu of the certificate which has been lost or destroyed, or has been mutilated, defaced or damaged so as to impair its value, while in possession of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

[Part Authority: Authority:72 Stat. 1114; 38 U.S.C. 501. Rights and benefits are continued in effect by sec. 12(b), 72 Stat. 1264, 38 U.S.C. note prec. Part 1.]

[Part Source: Source:13 FR 7122, Nov. 27, 1948, unless otherwise noted.]
