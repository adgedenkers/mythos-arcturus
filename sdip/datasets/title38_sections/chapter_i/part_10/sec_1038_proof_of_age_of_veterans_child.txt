TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 10: PART 10—ADJUSTED COMPENSATION
SECTION: § 10.38 Proof of age of veteran's child.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.12.0.148.25

============================================================

A child of a veteran shall be required to submit proof of age in accordance with the requirements set forth in the regulations of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

[Part Authority: Authority:72 Stat. 1114; 38 U.S.C. 501. Rights and benefits are continued in effect by sec. 12(b), 72 Stat. 1264, 38 U.S.C. note prec. Part 1.]

[Part Source: Source:13 FR 7122, Nov. 27, 1948, unless otherwise noted.]
