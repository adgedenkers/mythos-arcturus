CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 3: PART 3—ADJUDICATION
SUBPART A: Subpart A—Pension, Compensation, and Dependency and Indemnity Compensation
SECTION: § 3.60 Definition of “living with”.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.4.1.58.45

============================================================

For the purposes of determining entitlement to pension under 38 U.S.C. 1521, a person shall be considered as living with his or her spouse even though they reside apart unless they are estranged.

[Section Authority: (Authority: 38 U.S.C. 1521(h)(2))]
