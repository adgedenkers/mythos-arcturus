TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 3: PART 3—ADJUDICATION
SUBPART A: Subpart A—Pension, Compensation, and Dependency and Indemnity Compensation
SECTION: § 3.454 Apportionment of pension.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.4.1.70.188

============================================================

(a) Disability pension. Disability pension will be apportioned to the veteran's spouse or child or children.

(b) Survivors pension. Old-Law survivors pension, section 306 survivors pension, and improved pension will be apportioned to the veteran's child or children.
