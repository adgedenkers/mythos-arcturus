CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 3: PART 3—ADJUDICATION
SUBPART A: Subpart A—Pension, Compensation, and Dependency and Indemnity Compensation
SECTION: § 3.710 Civil service annuitants.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.4.1.74.230

============================================================

Department of Veterans Affairs benefits may be paid concurrently with civil service retirement benefits. However, payments will be considered income as provided in § 3.262 (e) and (h).
