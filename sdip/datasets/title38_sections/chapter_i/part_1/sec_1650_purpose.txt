TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 1: PART 1—GENERAL PROVISIONS
SECTION: § 1.650 Purpose.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.2.0.17.109

============================================================

The purpose of these regulations is to prescribe the procedure to be followed in determining and protecting the respective rights of the United States Government and of Department of Veterans Affairs employees who make inventions.

[Citation: [21 FR 10377, Dec. 28, 1956, as amended at 61 FR 29658, June 12, 1996]]
