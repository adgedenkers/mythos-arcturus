TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 1: PART 1—GENERAL PROVISIONS
SECTION: § 1.904 Form of payment.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.2.0.22.147

============================================================

Claims may be paid in the form of money or, when a contractual basis exists, VA may demand the return of specific property or the performance of specific services.
