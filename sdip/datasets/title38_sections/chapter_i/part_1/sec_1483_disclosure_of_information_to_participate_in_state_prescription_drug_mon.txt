TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 1: PART 1—GENERAL PROVISIONS
SECTION: § 1.483 Disclosure of information to participate in state prescription drug monitoring programs.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.2.0.10.37

============================================================

Information covered by §§ 1.460 through 1.499 of this part may be disclosed to State Prescription Drug Monitoring Programs pursuant to the limitations set forth in § 1.515 of this part.
