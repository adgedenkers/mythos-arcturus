TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 1: PART 1—GENERAL PROVISIONS
SECTION: § 1.897 Exceptions.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.2.0.21.142

============================================================

The Secretary of Veterans Affairs, or designees, may except positions from inclusion in this program as necessary to carry out the mission of the Department.
