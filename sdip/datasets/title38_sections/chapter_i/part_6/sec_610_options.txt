TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 6: PART 6—UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT LIFE INSURANCE
SECTION: § 6.10 Options.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.7.0.119.10

============================================================

Insurance will be payable in one sum only when selected by the insured during his or her lifetime or by his or her last will and testament.

[Citation: [61 FR 29025, June 7, 1996]]
