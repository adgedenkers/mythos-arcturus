TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 6: PART 6—UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT LIFE INSURANCE
SECTION: § 6.12 Special dividends.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.7.0.120.12

============================================================

Any special U.S. Government Life Insurance dividend that may be declared shall be paid in cash. Such special dividends shall not be accepted to accumulate on deposit or as a dividend credit.
