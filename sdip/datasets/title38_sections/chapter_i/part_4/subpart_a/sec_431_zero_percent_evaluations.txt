TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 4: PART 4—SCHEDULE FOR RATING DISABILITIES
SUBPART A: Subpart A—General Policy in Rating
SECTION: § 4.31 Zero percent evaluations.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.5.1.100.27

============================================================

In every instance where the schedule does not provide a zero percent evaluation for a diagnostic code, a zero percent evaluation shall be assigned when the requirements for a compensable evaluation are not met.
