TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 4: PART 4—SCHEDULE FOR RATING DISABILITIES
SUBPART B: Subpart B—Disability Ratings
SECTION: § 4.62 Circulatory disturbances.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.5.2.100.16

============================================================

The circulatory disturbances, especially of the lower extremity following injury in the popliteal space, must not be overlooked, and require rating generally as phlebitis.
