TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 4: PART 4—SCHEDULE FOR RATING DISABILITIES
SUBPART B: Subpart B—Disability Ratings
SECTION: § 4.61 Examination.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.5.2.100.15

============================================================

With any form of arthritis (except traumatic arthritis) it is essential that the examination for rating purposes cover all major joints, with especial reference to Heberden's or Haygarth's nodes.
