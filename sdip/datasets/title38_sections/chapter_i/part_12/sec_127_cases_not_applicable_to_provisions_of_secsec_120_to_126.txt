TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 12: PART 12—DISPOSITION OF VETERAN'S PERSONAL FUNDS AND EFFECTS
SECTION: § 12.7 Cases not applicable to provisions of §§ 12.0 to 12.6.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.14.0.155.8

============================================================

The provisions of §§ 12.0 to 12.6 shall be inapplicable to property known to be that of any person dying in or discharged or absent without leave from a Department of Veterans Affairs field facility other than a veteran admitted as such to such field facility.

[Citation: [13 FR 7129, Nov. 27, 1948]]

[Part Authority: Authority:38 U.S.C. 501, 8501-8528.]
