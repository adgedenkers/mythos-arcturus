TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 8: PART 8—NATIONAL SERVICE LIFE INSURANCE
SECTION: § 8.18 Total disability—speech.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.9.0.138.19

============================================================

The organic loss of speech shall be deemed to be total disability under National Service Life Insurance.

[Citation: [67 FR 54738, Aug. 26, 2002]]

[Part Authority: Authority:38 U.S.C. 501, 1901-1929, 1981-1988, unless otherwise noted.]
