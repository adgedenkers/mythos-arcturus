TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 8: PART 8—NATIONAL SERVICE LIFE INSURANCE
SECTION: § 8.25 Options.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.9.0.143.26

============================================================

Insurance will be paid in a lump sum only when selected by the insured during his or her lifetime or by his or her last will and testament.
