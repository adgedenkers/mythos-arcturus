TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 8: PART 8—NATIONAL SERVICE LIFE INSURANCE
SECTION: § 8.20 Proof of death, age, relationship and marriage.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.9.0.140.21

============================================================

Whenever it is necessary for a claimant to prove death, age, relationship or marriage, the provisions found in Part 3 of this chapter will be followed.
