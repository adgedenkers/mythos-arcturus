TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 16: PART 16—PROTECTION OF HUMAN SUBJECTS
SECTION: § 16.112 Review by Institution
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.18.0.169.11

============================================================

Research covered by this policy that has been approved by an IRB may be subject to further appropriate review and approval or disapproval by officials of the institution. However, those officials may not approve the research if it has not been approved by an IRB.

[Part Authority: Authority:5 U.S.C. 301; 38 U.S.C. 501, 7331, 7334; 42 U.S.C. 300v-1(b).]

[Part Source: Source:82 FR 7272, Jan. 19, 2017, unless otherwise noted.]
