TITLE 1: Title 38—Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief--Volume 1
CHAPTER I: CHAPTER I—DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS
PART 9: PART 9—SERVICEMEMBERS' GROUP LIFE INSURANCE AND VETERANS' GROUP LIFE INSURANCE
SECTION: § 9.6 Assignments.
NODE: 38:1.0.1.1.11.0.148.6

============================================================

Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance, Veterans' Group Life Insurance and benefits thereunder are not assignable.
