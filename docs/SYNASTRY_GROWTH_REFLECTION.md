# Rebuilding Connection After Conflict

Even healthy marriages experience conflict. What distinguishes resilient relationships is the ability to repair connection afterward.

A simple framework for repair includes:

1. **Pause** – allow emotions to settle before continuing discussion.
2. **Acknowledge** – recognize the other person's feelings.
3. **Reflect** – express understanding of their perspective.
4. **Reconnect** – reaffirm commitment to the relationship.

Repair conversations might begin with statements such as:

> "I want to understand what you experienced in that moment."

or

> "I care more about us than about being right."

These approaches transform conflict into opportunities for deeper understanding.
