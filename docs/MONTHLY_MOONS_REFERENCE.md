# Monthly Moons Across World Systems — Ontology Reference

## Overview

This ontology captures named monthly moons from **10 cultural/spiritual traditions** plus **8 special moon types**. Each tradition reflects its own ecology, cosmology, and relationship with the lunar cycle.

**Key insight**: Lunar months do NOT map 1:1 to Gregorian months. The Gregorian alignment is approximate. Most of these systems predate the Gregorian calendar by centuries or millennia.

## Quick Cross-Reference by Gregorian Month

### January
- **Algonquin**: Wolf Moon — wolves howling in deep winter
- **Ojibwe**: Spirit Moon (Minado Giizis) — spiritual renewal
- **Lakota**: Moon of Frost in the Tipi — frost inside dwellings
- **Celtic**: Quiet Moon — stillness before the cycle
- **Hindu**: Pushya Purnima — nourishment, sacred bathing
- **Chinese**: 12th Month / Sacrificial Month (腊月 Làyuè) — ancestor offerings
- **Sri Lankan**: Duruthu Poya — Buddha's first visit to Lanka

### February
- **Algonquin**: Snow Moon — heavy snowfall
- **Ojibwe**: Bear Moon (Makwa Giizis) — bears in deep hibernation
- **Lakota**: Moon When the Coyotes Are Frightened
- **Celtic**: Moon of Ice — endurance, inner fire
- **Hindu**: Magha Purnima — extremely auspicious, Ganges bathing
- **Chinese**: 1st Month / Capital Month (正月 Zhēngyuè) — Lunar New Year
- **Sri Lankan**: Navam Poya — appointment of chief disciples

### March
- **Algonquin**: Worm Moon — earthworms in thawing ground
- **Ojibwe**: Snow Crust Moon (Onaabidin Giizis) — freeze-thaw crust
- **Lakota**: Moon of the Snowblind — sun on snow
- **Celtic**: Seed Moon — planting time (Ostara)
- **Hindu**: Phalguna Purnima — Holika Dahan, Holi festival
- **Chinese**: 2nd Month / Apricot Month (杏月 Xìngyuè)
- **Sri Lankan**: Madin Poya — Buddha's return to father's palace

### April
- **Algonquin**: Pink Moon — wild ground phlox blooms
- **Ojibwe**: Broken Snowshoe Moon (Popogami Giizis) — wet heavy snow
- **Lakota**: Moon When Streams Are Navigable — ice breakup
- **Celtic**: Growing Moon — nurturing what's planted
- **Hindu**: Chaitra Purnima — Hindu new year, Hanuman Jayanti
- **Chinese**: 3rd Month / Peach Month (桃月 Táoyuè) — Qingming Festival
- **Sri Lankan**: Bak Poya — Buddha's second visit to Lanka

### May
- **Algonquin**: Flower Moon — abundance of blooming
- **Ojibwe**: Sucker Moon (Nimebine Giizis) — fish spawning runs
- **Lakota**: Moon When the Ponies Shed — horses shedding winter coats
- **Celtic**: Flower Moon — full bloom (Beltane)
- **Hindu**: Vaishakh Purnima — Buddha Purnima / Vesak
- **Chinese**: 4th Month / Plum Flower Month (梅月 Méiyuè)
- **Sri Lankan**: Vesak Poya — most sacred (birth/enlightenment/passing of Buddha)

### June
- **Algonquin**: Strawberry Moon — berry harvest
- **Ojibwe**: Blooming Moon (Waabigonii Giizis)
- **Lakota**: Moon of the June Berries (Wípazuka Wasté Wí)
- **Celtic**: Mead Moon — honey harvest, celebration (Litha)
- **Hindu**: Jyeshtha Purnima — the "eldest" month
- **Chinese**: 5th Month / Pomegranate Month (榴月 Liúyuè) — Dragon Boat Festival
- **Māori**: Pipiri — Matariki rises, NEW YEAR begins

### July
- **Algonquin**: Buck Moon — antler growth
- **Ojibwe**: Berry Moon (Miin Giizis)
- **Lakota**: Moon When Chokecherries Are Ripe — Sun Dance season
- **Celtic**: Horse Moon — sovereignty, power
- **Hindu**: Ashadh Purnima — GURU PURNIMA (Shiva as Adi Guru)
- **Chinese**: 6th Month / Lotus Month (荷月 Héyuè)
- **Sri Lankan**: Esala Poya — Buddha's first sermon, Kandy Perahera

### August
- **Algonquin**: Sturgeon Moon — Great Lakes sturgeon migration
- **Ojibwe**: Grain Moon / Wild Rice (Minoomini Giizis) — sacred wild rice harvest
- **Lakota**: Moon of the Harvest (Wasúton Wí)
- **Celtic**: Grain Moon — first harvest (Lughnasadh / Lammas)
- **Hindu**: Shravan Purnima — Raksha Bandhan (sibling bond)
- **Chinese**: 7th Month / Orchid Month (兰月 Lányuè) — Ghost Month
- **Sri Lankan**: Nikini Poya — First Buddhist Council

### September
- **Algonquin**: Corn Moon — corn harvest (may be Harvest Moon)
- **Ojibwe**: Changing Leaves Moon (Wabaabagaa Giizis)
- **Lakota**: Moon When Calves Grow Hair
- **Celtic**: Singing Moon — harvest songs (Mabon)
- **Hindu**: Bhadrapad Purnima — Uma Maheshvara Vrata
- **Chinese**: 8th Month / Osmanthus Month (桂月 Guìyuè) — MID-AUTUMN FESTIVAL
- **Sri Lankan**: Binara Poya — establishment of the Bhikkhuni order

### October
- **Algonquin**: Hunter's Moon — peak hunting season
- **Ojibwe**: Falling Leaves Moon (Binaakwe Giizis)
- **Lakota**: Moon of the Changing Season
- **Celtic**: Seed Fall Moon — death feeding rebirth (Samhain)
- **Hindu**: Ashvin/Sharad Purnima — moon radiates healing nectar, Raas Leela
- **Chinese**: 9th Month / Chrysanthemum Month (菊月 Júyuè) — Double Ninth
- **Sri Lankan**: Vap Poya — Kathina robe-offering season begins

### November
- **Algonquin**: Beaver Moon — beavers preparing for winter
- **Ojibwe**: Freezing Moon (Baashkaakodin Giizis)
- **Lakota**: Moon When Deer Shed Antlers
- **Celtic**: Dark Moon — veil thinnest, ancestors walk
- **Hindu**: Kartik Purnima — Dev Deepavali (gods celebrate Diwali)
- **Chinese**: 10th Month / Positive Month (阳月 Yángyuè) — yang returns hidden
- **Sri Lankan**: Ill Poya — 60 monks dispatched to spread Dhamma

### December
- **Algonquin**: Cold Moon — winter's grip, longest nights
- **Ojibwe**: Little Spirit Moon (Minado Giisoonhs)
- **Lakota**: Moon of the Popping Trees — trees crack from cold
- **Celtic**: Oak Moon — Oak King at Yule, return of light
- **Hindu**: Margashirsha Purnima — Datta Jayanti, Krishna's "best month"
- **Chinese**: 11th Month / Winter Month (冬月 Dōngyuè) — Winter Solstice (Dōngzhì)
- **Sri Lankan**: Unduvap Poya — Bodhi Tree sapling arrives in Lanka

## Special Moon Types

| Type | Definition | Frequency | Magical Significance |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Blue Moon** | 2nd full moon in a calendar month, OR 3rd of 4 in a season | ~2.5 years | Amplified magic, impossible goals, wild card |
| **Black Moon** | 2nd new moon in a month, OR month with no full moon | ~29 months | Deep shadow work, void magic, banishing |
| **Supermoon** | Full moon at/near perigee (closest to Earth) | 3-4/year | Heightened intuition, amplified emotions |
| **Micromoon** | Full moon at/near apogee (farthest from Earth) | 3-4/year | Subtle energy, inner work, quiet reflection |
| **Blood Moon** | Total lunar eclipse (reddish color) | 2-3/year | Massive transformation, karmic culmination |
| **Harvest Moon** | Full moon closest to autumn equinox | 1/year | Reaping, gratitude, abundance |
| **Hunter's Moon** | First full moon after Harvest Moon | 1/year | Pursuit, provision, calling in what you need |
| **Void of Course** | Moon between last aspect and sign change | Multiple/month | Don't start new things; meditate, rest, release |

## Queries

```sql
-- All moon names for current month
SELECT * FROM v_current_month_moons;

-- Compare all traditions for February
SELECT * FROM v_moons_by_month WHERE gregorian_month_approx = 2;

-- Find all moons with harvest/abundance themes
SELECT system_key, moon_name, meaning 
FROM monthly_moons 
WHERE meaning ILIKE '%harvest%' OR meaning ILIKE '%abundance%';

-- All Purnima moons with their festivals
SELECT moon_name, meaning, festivals, deity_association 
FROM monthly_moons 
WHERE system_key = 'hindu_purnima' 
ORDER BY month_order;
```

## Neo4j Integration Notes

For the Mythos graph database, these can be loaded as:
- `(:MoonName {name, system, meaning, ...})` nodes
- `(:NamingSystem {key, name, tradition, ...})` nodes
- `(:MoonName)-[:BELONGS_TO]->(:NamingSystem)` relationships
- `(:MoonName)-[:APPROXIMATE_MONTH]->(month_num)` for cross-referencing
- `(:MoonName)-[:ASSOCIATED_WITH]->(:Deity)` for divine connections
- `(:MoonName)-[:CELEBRATED_AT]->(:Festival)` for ceremony links
