## Definition

**Soul Stratigraphy** is a tri-field astrological analysis method that examines a person's birth chart through three distinct civilizational lenses, then synthesizes them into a fourth unified reading of the incarnated soul.

---

## The Three Strata

### Stratum I — Hellenistic (The Bedrock)
- **System:** Whole sign houses, traditional seven planets, sect, lots (Fortune, Spirit, Eros), time-lord techniques (profections, zodiacal releasing)
- **Domain:** Fate, fortune, and the soul's contract with time
- **What it reveals:** The bones of the incarnation — what was fated, what was given, what the daimon assigned. This is the oldest layer, the one closest to the soul's entry point into matter.
- **Key techniques:** Sect analysis, essential dignities, Lot of Fortune and Spirit, annual profections, zodiacal releasing from Spirit, triplicity lords of the sect light, phasis, fixed stars on angles

### Stratum II — Vedic / Jyotish (The Karmic Layer)
- **System:** Sidereal zodiac, nakshatras, Vimshottari dasha, divisional charts (D1 through D60), Ashtakavarga, Shadbala
- **Domain:** Karma, dharma, and the soul's trajectory across incarnations
- **What it reveals:** The karmic inheritance — what the soul carries from prior lives, what debts are active, what dharmic path is encoded. This is the layer of cosmic accounting.
- **Key techniques:** Nakshatra analysis, Vimshottari dasha periods, Navamsha (D9) for dharma and marriage, Dashamsha (D10) for career, yogas, Rahu-Ketu axis as karmic vector, Shadbala planetary strength

### Stratum III — Western Tropical (The Psychological Surface)
- **System:** Tropical zodiac, Placidus houses, outer planets (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto), asteroids, Chiron
- **Domain:** Personality, psychological growth, and the ego's relationship to the unconscious
- **What it reveals:** The personality structure — how the soul expresses through the ego, what psychological patterns emerge, where growth and wounding intersect. This is the most visible layer, the one the world sees.
- **Key techniques:** Aspect patterns, house rulership chains, Chiron wound/gift axis, outer planet generational signatures, midpoints, composite and synastry for relationships

## The Fourth Field — The Synthesis

The synthesis is not an average of the three readings. It is what emerges when you overlay them like archaeological strata in a trench profile:

- **Where all three agree** = Bedrock truth about the soul. Non-negotiable. This is what the incarnation IS.
- **Where two agree and one diverges** = The tension that defines specific life work. The divergent system reveals the domain where the soul is being stretched.
- **Where all three diverge** = The frontier. Unresolved territory. Often the site of the soul's most transformative potential — and its deepest confusion.

The synthesis should be read like a stratigraphic column:
1. Start from the bottom (Hellenistic/fate)
2. Read upward through the middle (Vedic/karma)
3. Arrive at the surface (Western/psychology)
4. Then describe the full column as a unified formation

## Notes on Rectification

When birth time is uncertain (Rodden Rating C or DD), the rectification process should be documented within the report. The Hellenistic stratum is most sensitive to birth time (angles, lots, profections all depend on it). The Vedic stratum is second most sensitive (lagna, house placements, dasha balance). The Western tropical stratum is least sensitive for planetary sign positions but highly sensitive for house placements.

When rectifying for a Soul Stratigraphy, all three systems should be tested against known life events to converge on a single birth time that satisfies all three frameworks.
