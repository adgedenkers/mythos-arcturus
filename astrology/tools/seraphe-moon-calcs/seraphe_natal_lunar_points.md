## MONTHLY RHYTHM

The Moon moves ~13° per day and completes a full cycle every 27.3 days. Each point above gets activated by conjunction once per month, and by every other aspect type once per month as well.

**Her key monthly windows:**

- **11°–16° Pisces** (IC through natal Moon): Her monthly emotional reset. Maximum permeability. 2-day descent into the deepest part of her chart. The Magdalene channel opens widest here.
- **14°–16° Sagittarius** (square Moon + conjunct Neptune): Her monthly psychic flood. Boundary dissolution, can't distinguish her feelings from others'.
- **14°–16° Virgo** (opposite Moon + square Neptune): The most disorienting transit — overwhelm meets self-criticism meets psychic confusion.
- **14° Libra** (conjunct Pluto): Monthly Pluto activation — ancestral material, power dynamics, deep purging.
- **9°–12° Libra** (Mars then Venus): The whiplash window — frustration into sweetness within 24 hours.
- **2–3 days after each Full Moon**: Her native phase (Disseminating). She's most in rhythm here — the teacher/transmitter mode activates naturally.
- **New Moon days**: Can feel hollow, disconnected, like the current is shut off.
